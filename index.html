Here are a few things (mostly on the programming side) that I’ve worked on in the last 2 years.

AutoLocate

AutoLocate is research on privacy leaks in GIS-rendered maps. I built an image-processing pipeline and geometric optimization methods to recover map-marker locations from published figures. I co-authored a paper on this work that was accepted to ACM CCS 2026, a top-tier security conference.

Python · PIL

I revived Purdue Math Club’s Problem of the Week, a tradition that had been discontinued before I joined, and built a site to share interesting problems with other students. I’m working with the Purdue math department to expand its reach.

Connect 4

I built a web app where you can play Connect 4 with a friend. I’m also researching how humans can play perfectly: instead of checking thousands of positions per move, I’m working on a compressed strategy that is followable and memorizable, with about 10,000 positions. I plan to add a trainer to the site that goes over the perfect strategy.

React · Node.js · PostgreSQL

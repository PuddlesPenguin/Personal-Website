Connect 4

I’m a really big Connect 4 geek and can play near perfectly. I made a web app where you can play Connect 4 with a friend. On the side, I’ve also been doing self-research on how a human can play perfectly.

React · Node.js · PostgreSQL

Connect 4 is a solved game by computers: they achieve it by checking thousands of positions per move, especially in the early game. I did a mini self-driven research project on making a compressed strategy that is followable and memorizable by humans, where you only have to memorize about 10,000 positions. I plan to add a trainer to the site that goes over the perfect strategy.
